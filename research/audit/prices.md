A company changes rows 53 times while a member (a new ticker, or a new listing). At 9 of them the new file has no bar for the previous session, or another security's (Alpaca's histories follow a symbol), so that day's return is stitched from the previous row's close. A stitched return assumes no corporate action that day; `reference/price_events.csv` corrects the ones that had one.
