# Price audit

Generated by `python -m data_pipeline.audit_prices` on 2026-11-05. Source: Alpaca daily bars (SIP), raw and fully adjusted, fetched per company and ticker by `python -m data_pipeline.bars` from the identity table: 787 plan rows for 719 companies. Checks run to 2026-10-28, the last bar of the files reused from the first download. Company member days to then: 1,360,471 (a company listed with two classes counts once a day), 64 of them without a bar.

A company changes rows 56 times while a member (a new ticker, or a new listing). At 11 of them the new file has no bar for the previous session, or another security's (Alpaca's histories follow a symbol), so that day's return is stitched from the previous row's close. A stitched return assumes no corporate action that day; `reference/price_events.csv` corrects the ones that had one. A company's first member day, or a return after a gap in its membership, has no previous row to stitch from (208 days). At 195 of them the file's previous bar can't be shown to be the company's own (see `returns`), so that day's return is left undefined, raw and adjusted alike.
