## Session recaps against their timestamps (created_at in New York time)

Recaps are Benzinga's lists of a session's movers, with the day and the session named in the headline. Windows: pre-market 4:00 to 9:30, mid-day 11:00 to 14:00, after-hours 16:00 to 20:00. A late stamp only delays a story. An early one, before the session began, would leak the future into the past, so early is the column that matters.
